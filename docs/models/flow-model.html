Step 2: the flow model (mask to image)

Turns a segmentation mask and random noise into a synthetic cardiac MR image. A diffusion-style model, and the current image model of the project.

Models: Mask model (step 1) · Flow model (step 2) · SPADE GAN (baseline)

What it does

The model draws a short-axis cardiac MR slice that follows a given mask: the left ventricle, the muscle wall and the right ventricle sit where the mask says. The rest of the picture (chest wall, other organs, texture) is made up by the model.

Input data

  • Real data: pairs of a real image and its real mask from the 85 training patients, ED and ES, 128 × 128 pixels, 1,587 slices.
  • At generation time: a mask (real or made by the step 1 model) and random noise. No real image is used.
  • Split: 85 training and 15 validation patients, split by patient. The original test sets are not used.

How it was trained

  • Flow matching: take a real image and a noise image, pick a point on the straight line between them, and train the network to predict the direction from noise to image, given the point, the time and the mask.
  • In 10% of the training steps the mask is hidden. The network therefore also learns to draw without a mask, and at generation the two predictions are combined (“guidance”) so that the image follows the mask more strictly.
  • An averaged copy of the weights (EMA) is used for generation. 16,000 steps on a free GPU.

Architecture

A U-Net with residual blocks and attention at the coarsest level, 24.7 million parameters. The mask is resized and given to every block, not only to the first layer, so the heart outline is not forgotten deeper in the network. To generate, the model starts from a noise image and takes 50 small steps in the predicted direction.

Visual results

  • The synthetic images look like cardiac MR slices, with a clear muscle wall and plausible surrounding anatomy.
  • The image follows the mask it is given. Where the synthetic mask is broken (DCM, HCM), the image made from it is broken too.
  • Compared with the SPADE GAN baseline the images are sharper and more varied. The scores are in Results.

Limits

  • One training run on 15 validation patients: a repeat with other seeds is still needed.
  • With only 1,587 training slices the model could memorise training images. A first nearest-neighbour and copy check found no sign of copying. Membership-inference tests are still to do.
  • The guidance setting was compared on the validation patients, so the best score is slightly optimistic.
