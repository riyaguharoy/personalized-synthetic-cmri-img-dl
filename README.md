# Clinical-Profile-Conditioned Synthetic Cardiac MRI(Magnetic Resonance Imaging) Generation Using Deep Generative Models
Develop deep generative models to produce realistic, diverse, and anatomically consistent Clinical-Profile-Conditioned synthetic cardiac MRI images. The project will explore GAN-, VAE-, and diffusion-based approaches, with evaluation focused on image fidelity, anatomical consistency, diversity, privacy and the usefulness of synthetic data for downstream cardiac image analysis.
A feasible clinical-profile-conditioned approach for generating synthetic cardiac MR images together with corresponding cardiac-structure segmentation masks, providing finer control over the generation process using suitable clinical information available in the selected dataset. An evaluation of whether the generated cardiac MR images and corresponding segmentation masks preserve plausible cardiac anatomy and reflect the characteristics used for conditioning. A comparison between the clinical-profile-conditioned approach and a suitable baseline in terms of fidelity, downstream utility, and privacy.
Together, these contributions will also provide a clearer understanding of the possible benefits, limitations, and trade-offs of using clinical-profile information in synthetic cardiac MRI generation.

The thesis does not assume that clinical-profile conditioning will improve every evaluation measure. Negative or mixed findings will also be useful because they can show where this type of conditioning works, where it does not, and what needs further investigation.
